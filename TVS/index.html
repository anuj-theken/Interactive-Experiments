    Chapter 01

    Nails on the road

    How a bus service between Madurai, Thanjavur and Pudukkottai fought a problem nobody else saw.

    Scroll to follow the route

    Horse-drawn and bullock carts were still common on the mostly unpaved roads of India, and nails loosened and fell out of the animals’ hooves.

    These nails, left on the road, punctured tyres and regularly caused automobile breakdowns.

    At the time, TV Sundaram was running a bus service between Madurai, Thanjavur, and Pudukkottai.

    He had invested Rs 50,000, basically all his savings and profits from the previous venture, into this business. Busted buses could ruin him.

    To solve his nail problem, Sundaram paid boys to walk his routes and collect nails and glass bottles.

    But the carts kept coming, and the nails kept falling.

    Years later, he fitted magnets under the buses to pick up stray nails.

    Chennai, Tamil Nadu

    Most had never left Tamil Nadu

    In 1992, TVS employees were set to train at Suzuki in Hamamatsu, Japan. For many of them it would be the first journey beyond their home state.

    The travellers

    Three batches of 25

    Seventy-five employees in all, travelling to Japan in three separate groups.

    Outbound

    East across the Bay of Bengal

    Each batch made the trip from Chennai to Hamamatsu, on the Pacific coast of Shizuoka Prefecture.

    Three to four months at Suzuki

    Each batch trained at Suzuki’s headquarters for three to four months, far from home and in a new working culture.

    Homebound

    Market share · Electric two-wheelers

    TVS now leads India’s electric two-wheeler market

    TVS Motor’s share rose from 4% in FY22 to 26% in CY26, as Ola Electric fell from 35% at its FY24 peak to under 7%.

    FY22

    Hero Electric led; TVS had 4%

    India’s electric two-wheeler market was led by Hero Electric with 27% and Okinawa with 19%. TVS Motor had 4%.

    FY24

    Ola Electric surged to 35%

    Ola Electric took the largest share of any company. TVS Motor had climbed to 19%, while Hero Electric was down to 1%.

    CY26

    TVS leads with 26%

    TVS Motor is the market leader, ahead of Bajaj Auto at 22%. Ola Electric has fallen to under 7%.
