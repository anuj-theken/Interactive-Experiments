Nifty 50

2007

The Split

The fight drags on. Family members agreed in 2003 to settle, but a stock market rally led to a surge in value, causing the agreement to stall as the stakes became too valuable to trade.

2008

Global Financial Crisis

Bajaj Auto is hit hard as markets crash. While both companies suffer, Bajaj Auto's core manufacturing fundamentals remain intact despite the 60% market dip.

2009

The Recovery

Bajaj Auto rebounds strongly on two-wheeler demand. Meanwhile, Bajaj Hindustan begins to struggle with sugar sector headwinds and regulatory pressure.

2013

The Nadir

Bajaj Hindustan hits its lowest point—just 6.8% of its 2007 value. The sugar sector faces severe margin pressure, marking a stark contrast to Auto's climb.

2014

The Modi Wave

Market optimism ignites. Bajaj Auto rallies sharply on rural demand and exports, while Bajaj Hindustan remains mired in debt and structural issues.

2023 - 2025

The Great Divergence

Bajaj Auto surpasses 13x its 2007 value. Bajaj Hindustan remains near zero—a cautionary tale of two different fates within the same family empire.
