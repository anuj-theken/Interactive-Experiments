Data Accuracy

Is this actual Bajaj Finance data?

Partially accurate — it's a realistic approximation, not an official published rate. The interest on no-cost EMI is subsidised by the merchant via a "subvention" discount that Bajaj charges retailers. The actual rate varies by product category, brand, and tenure and is a private B2B arrangement — never publicly disclosed.

Calculation Method

Formula used in this calculator

This tool uses a flat / simple interest model for clarity. It's easier to understand but slightly overstates the actual cost compared to the bank method.

Total Interest= Principal × (Annual Rate ÷ 12) × Tenure
Monthly EMI= Principal ÷ Tenure
Retailer's Loss= Total Interest (paid as subvention)
🏦 Banks use a reducing-balance formula: EMI = P × r(1+r)ⁿ ÷ ((1+r)ⁿ−1). The flat model makes the retailer's loss appear ~20–30% larger than it truly is.

Key Concept

What is Effective Cost %?

Effective Cost % is the total subvention as a share of the product price — how much of the selling price the retailer surrenders to Bajaj to fund your "free" EMI.

Effective Cost %= (Total Interest ÷ Principal) × 100
