2003

The Agreement

The fight drags on. The family members came to an agreement in 2003 with Sharad Pawar and S Gurumurthy as mediators, and decided to buy each other's stakes in companies. But a stock market rally leads to the value of Bajaj Auto and Bajaj Hindusthan to rise, and the agreement stalls.

2007

Bajaj Auto & Finserv

Acquired a 14.5% stake in KTM Power Sports AG, which gave Bajaj entry into the global premium motorcycle segment. Demerger of Bajaj Auto and Bajaj Finserv Ltd. Bajaj Hindustan undertook aggressive greenfield capacity expansion making it India's largest ethanol producer by 2007.

2019 - 2020

Chetak EV

Re-entry into scooters with the Chetak EV (unveiled electric Chetak in October 2019 and launched it commercially in January 2020).

2021

Market Cap Milestone

Became the first two-wheeler company in the world to cross Rs 1 lakh crore market capitalization.

2022 - 2023

Debt & Restructuring

SBI filed an NCLT petition in 2022, which was withdrawn in 2023 following a debt-restructuring scheme (insolvency proceedings and debt restructuring).
