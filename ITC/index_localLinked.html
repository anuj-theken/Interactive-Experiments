    Market share of Aashirvaad

    How ITC became a market dominator in just 8 years

    Aashirvaad Rest of market
    2015
    The organised wheat-flour segment was worth only about ₹3,500 crore — a sliver of India's ₹30,000 crore atta market. With few branded rivals, Aashirvaad held roughly 75% and owned most of it.
    2018
    As packaged atta caught on, the branded market expanded fast and fragmented. New entrants took slices, so Aashirvaad's share fell to about 28% even as its sales climbed past ₹4,200 crore.
    2026
    In a now-mature, much larger branded market, Aashirvaad pulled back to clear leadership at over 45%. Nearly half of a big category dwarfs three-quarters of the old small one.

    ITC FMCG brands

    The consumer products that ITC made became household names

    Scroll ↓
    July 2003

    Sunfeast

    At launch in 2003, Britannia and Parle held a combined market share of over 80%. By 2021, Sunfeast secured 7.30%. By 2023, Britannia stood at 31%, Parle at 29%, and ITC was designated inside the top three capturing over 70% together.

    March 2007

    ITC Bingo!

    Within 9 months of its 2007 launch, Bingo! captured approximately 16% market share. During this period, Frito-Lay (PepsiCo) held around 50%, while Haldiram's retained roughly 25% share.

    September 2010

    Sunfeast Yippee!

    By 2016, Nestlé Maggi led the category with more than 50% market share, while Yippee! achieved a 30% to 40% share. In 2026, Nestlé Maggi stands at 66% with Yippee! holding 22% share.
