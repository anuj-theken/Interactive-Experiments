July 2003

Sunfeast

At launch in 2003, Britannia and Parle held a combined market share of over 80%. By 2021, Sunfeast secured 7.30%. By 2023, Britannia stood at 31%, Parle at 29%, and ITC was designated inside the top three capturing over 70% together.

March 2007

ITC Bingo!

Within 9 months of its 2007 launch, Bingo! captured approximately 16% market share. During this period, Frito-Lay (PepsiCo) held around 50%, while Haldiram's retained roughly 25% share.

September 2010

Sunfeast Yippee!

By 2016, Nestlé Maggi led the category with more than 50% market share, while Yippee! achieved a 30% to 40% share. In 2026, Nestlé Maggi stands at 66% with Yippee! holding 22% share.
