About the data

301 readers of First Principles answered the survey between 13 and 22 September 2026. It is not a representative sample: 72% of respondents are men, and most live in Bengaluru, Mumbai or Delhi NCR. Your answers stay in your browser. They are not saved or sent anywhere.

Percentages leave out readers who skipped a question. If a reader said they expected their happiest decade to be “the one I'm in now”, we counted it as the decade they are in. Themes in the middle-age answers were assigned by keyword matching, so they are approximate. When your decade has fewer than 10 readers, we compare you with all readers instead. Characters are illustrations, not portraits of real readers.
