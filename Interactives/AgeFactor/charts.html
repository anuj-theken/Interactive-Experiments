The age factor
301 readers told us which decade was their best, when middle age caught up with them, and at what age they would stop the clock.
Survey of First Principles readers, 13–22 September 2026. Not a representative sample: 72% of respondents are men, and most live in Bengaluru, Mumbai or Delhi NCR.
Behind us, or still ahead?
Readers say their happiest decade so far was their 20s or 30s. Asked about the future, the most popular pick is their 50s.
