# Are your best years behind you? — text transcript

This file holds all the reader-facing text in the interactive (`index.html`), in reading order.

## How to edit

- **Edit only the text under each tag.** Keep every `<!-- id: … -->` tag exactly as it is. The tags are how your changes get matched back to the page.
- The lines starting with `> 🖼` describe what the chart or illustration shows at that moment. They're for context only. Edits there are ignored, but you can leave a comment for the graphics desk (see below).
- Formatting:
  - `**bold**` is bold.
  - `*italic*` is italic.
  - `==text==` is the coloured highlight used in the scrolly cards.
  - A line starting `Note:` is the small grey footnote under a card.
  - `Big number:` is the large figure at the top of a card.
- Text inside `{curly braces}` is filled in live from the reader's own answers, e.g. `{your decade}` → "your 30s". Move or reword around them freely, but keep the braces.
- **Numbers come from the data.** If you change a figure, flag it with a comment so the chart can be checked too.
- To leave a question or note instead of an edit, write `[ED: your note]` anywhere.
- Don't delete a block to cut it. Write `[CUT]` as its text.

## About the data

<!-- id: method.heading -->
About the data

<!-- id: method.p1 -->
301 readers of First Principles answered the survey between 13 and 22 September 2026. It is not a representative sample. 72% of respondents are men, and most live in Bengaluru, Mumbai or Delhi NCR.

<!-- id: method.p2 -->
Percentages leave out readers who skipped a question or said it was too close to call. If a reader said they expected their happiest decade to be “the one I'm in now”, we counted it as the decade they are in. Themes in the middle-age answers were assigned by keyword matching, so a single answer can count towards more than one theme. We quote only readers who agreed to be quoted, and identify them only by decade and city. The characters are illustrations, not portraits of real readers.
