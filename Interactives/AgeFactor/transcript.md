## 📊 / 🖼️ / 🔘 VISUAL — don't edit, context only

Descriptions of the things on the page that aren't text, so you know what the words sit next to:

- 📊 **INTERACTIVE CHART**: a data chart that moves as the reader scrolls. Readers can hover over or tap any square to see that reader's answers.
- 🖼️ **ILLUSTRATION**: a drawing (character, scene or lede image). No data.
- 🔘 **ANSWER INPUT**: the slider or buttons where the reader gives their own answer.

They look like this, in a grey box with a ⛔ line:

> 📊 **INTERACTIVE CHART** — ⛔ context only, don't edit
> **Chart type:** …
> **What changes at this step:** …

Anything you change inside these boxes is ignored. To ask for a change to a chart, write `[ED: …]` next to it.

## How the scroll sections work

Most sections are **scrollytelling**: a chart stays pinned on screen while text cards scroll up over it. Each card is one **Step**. When a new card arrives, the chart changes. So each Step below has:

1. 📊 what the chart shows at that moment, then
2. ✏️ the chart's title and subtitle (shown above the chart), and the card text.

## Formatting in TEXT blocks

| Write | To get |
|---|---|
| `**word**` | **bold** |
| `*word*` | *italic* |
| `==word==` | the coloured highlight used in cards |
| A line starting `Note:` | the small grey footnote at the bottom of a card |
| A line starting `Big number:` | the large red number at the top of a card |
| `{curly braces}` | filled in live from the reader's own answers, e.g. `{decade}` → "30s". Reword around them, but keep the braces. |

## Leaving notes

- `[ED: your note]` anywhere: a question or a request instead of an edit.
- `[CUT]` as the text: remove that block. Please don't delete blocks.
- If you change a **number**, add `[ED: number changed]` so the chart can be checked to match.

## ABOUT THE DATA

✏️ **TEXT · Section heading**
<!-- id: method.heading -->
About the data

✏️ **TEXT · Methodology paragraph 1**
<!-- id: method.p1 -->
301 readers of First Principles answered the survey between 13 and 22 September 2026. It is not a representative sample. 72% of respondents are men, and most live in Bengaluru, Mumbai or Delhi NCR.

✏️ **TEXT · Methodology paragraph 2**
<!-- id: method.p2 -->
Percentages leave out readers who skipped a question or said it was too close to call. If a reader said they expected their happiest decade to be “the one I'm in now”, we counted it as the decade they are in. Themes in the middle-age answers were assigned by keyword matching, so a single answer can count towards more than one theme. We quote only readers who agreed to be quoted, and identify them only by decade and city. The characters are illustrations, not portraits of real readers.
