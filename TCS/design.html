04

Chart palette

Validated — fixed order, never cycled

The moodboard's strip is a ramp, so it fails as a series palette: its ends are too pale, too low in chroma, and aqua sits too close to green to be told apart. These six slots keep the board's hues but are stepped into the legible band. Assign them in order — series 1 takes slot 1 — and never generate a seventh.

Held at ΔE ≥ 13.8 from the nearest categorical slot so a status colour can never read as "series 4". Always shipped with an icon and a label — never colour alone.

Rules that travel with this palette

One y-axis, always — two measures of different scale become two charts, never a second axis. Colour follows the entity, not its rank: a filter that drops series must not repaint the survivors. Values, labels and legends wear the ink tokens (--color-text / --color-muted), never the series colour. Scatter, bubble, choropleth and small-multiple forms cap at three series — beyond that, facet or fold into "Other". Keep a 2px surface gap between adjacent fills and stacked segments.

05

Motifs

The six devices the board repeats

The board is not a colour brief so much as a set of recurring devices. These are the ones that showed up often enough to be system, not decoration.

1968Tata Computer Centre opens
1974First offshore contract
1999Y2K remediation peak
Timeline spine
A 1px rule with cobalt nodes; years in mono, event copy in Archivo beneath. The board's most-used structure — five separate pieces use it.
+98% growth in offshore headcount over six years
Hero stat
One figure at display scale in cobalt, with a short caption under it. Replaces a chart whenever the story is a single number.
Isotype / waffle grid
Unit squares or figures, one per quantum, 10 to a row. Greige for the remainder, cobalt for the count, orange for the part being called out.
Archive · 1981
Duotone archival photo
Black-and-white archive imagery under a cobalt-to-navy multiply. Aqua caption sits on top. Keeps decades of mixed-quality photography looking like one set.
Graph paper ground
A 22px aqua grid on Bone, standing in for chart gridlines. Recessive — the grid never competes with the mark drawn over it.
Chapter 03 The bug that paid for a decade
Navy chapter panel
Full-bleed Ink Navy to break the cream run and mark a new act. Aqua eyebrow, Bone heading. Charts inside switch to the dark palette.

Cards are flat in this system — a hairline --color-rule border on white over the Bone ground. The board's references are printed pieces; the heavy 30px drop shadow from the previous house style does not belong here. Keep the frame wrapper anyway (see the prompt guide) — it owns the card's padding, and any module that does opt into a shadow still needs it.
