The Tata leadership power map

The Tata Group’s structure shows who commands authority through time. N Chandrasekaran is a rare individual who managed to rise through TCS and become the group chairman.

Tier 1

Tata Trusts

Tata Trusts is the group’s philanthropic umbrella and the majority shareholder of Tata Sons with a 66% stake. It includes the Sir Ratan Tata Trust and Sir Dorabji Tata Trust.

  • Noel TataCurrent

    Born 1957. Tata Trusts chairman since Oct 2024. Previously a candidate to chair Tata Sons.

Tier 2

Tata Sons

Tata Sons is the principal investment holding company and promoter of the Tata Group. It controls a 72% stake in TCS and appoints the group chairman.

Tier 2 · Tata Sons

  1. Jamsetji Tata

    Born 1839, died 1904. Founded the Tata Group in 1868.

  2. JRD Tata

    Born 1904, died 1993. Chairman of Tata Sons and the Tata Group from 1938 to 1991, then chairman emeritus until his death. Funded the data processing unit that eventually became TCS. The IPO of TCS was timed to his 100th birth anniversary.

  3. Ratan Tata

    Born 1937, died 2024. Tata Sons chairman from 1991 to Dec 2012. Interim chairman from 24 Oct 2016 to 21 Feb 2017. Led the 2004 IPO roadshow. Tapped N Chandrasekaran as Tata Sons chairman in Jan 2017.

  4. Cyrus MistryRemoved

    Chairman from Dec 2012 to 24 Oct 2016. Removed after losing the board’s confidence. Died 4 Sep 2022.

  5. N ChandrasekaranCurrent

    Chairman from 21 Feb 2017 to 20 Feb 2032 (pending shareholder approval at the next annual general meeting). Originally resigned in Aug 2026 but reversed the decision in the following month.

Tier 3

TCS

TCS is the Tata Group’s flagship operating company. TCS’s dividends fund much of the group’s operations.

Tier 3 · TCS

  1. PM Agarwala

    Originally the managing director of Tata Electric, took charge of TCS when it was a spin-off in 1968. Remained CEO until 1974.

  2. FC Kohli

    Joined TCS as general manager, then led the company from 1969 to 2000. Personally signed off on Chandrasekaran’s hiring. Known as the “father of the Indian software industry”.

  3. S Ramadorai

    Joined TCS in 1972. Worked as resident manager in New York in 1979. Led as CEO from 1996 to 2009. Set up the Y2K “software factory” and Ignite programme. Appointed Chandrasekaran as his executive assistant after becoming CEO.

  4. N Chandrasekaran

    Joined TCS as an intern in 1987. Rose through the ranks and was CEO from 2009 to 2017.

  5. Rajesh GopinathanRemoved

    Was CEO from 2017 to 2023. Abrupt exit amid restructuring.

  6. K KrithivasanCurrent

    TCS CEO from 2023 to the present. Facing GCCs, H-1B visa surcharges in the US, and pricing pressure due to artificial intelligence.
