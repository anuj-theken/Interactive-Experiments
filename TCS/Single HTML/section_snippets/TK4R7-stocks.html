Post-IPO Baseline & Financial Crisis

Following early listing milestones, the global financial crisis of 2008–2009 heavily suppressed valuation across Indian IT exporters, forcing consolidation.

2010 – 2013

TCS Pulls Ahead

TCS expands its margin advantage over industry peers, crossing 4.9x base valuation by 2013, supported by massive scaling in global enterprise services.

2014 – 2018

Digital & Cloud Migration Era

Large enterprise cloud transformation contracts spark multi-year multi-fold growth. TCS surges to over 12x its 2006 baseline while HCLTech steadily gains momentum.

2019 – 2022

Post-Pandemic Digital Surge

Unprecedented demand for remote work technology, cloud infrastructure, and software digitalization propels TCS above 32x and Infosys above 15x base growth.

Peak Expansion & Normalization

TCS touches a record 39x expansion in 2025 before industry growth normalizes in 2026 amid global macroeconomic stabilization.
